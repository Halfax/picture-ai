To achieve high-end photorealism with your current SDXL models, you should pair them with specific LoRAs (Low-Rank Adaptations) designed to add "grit," skin texture, and realistic lighting.
Since you are using Juggernaut-XL v9 and RealVisXL V5.0, these are the best hyper-realistic LoRAs currently favored by the community:
Top Hyper-Realism LoRAs for SDXL
UltraRealistic Lora V2: This is a top-tier choice for making images indistinguishable from actual or analog photos. It adds a layer of "raw" photography feel that works perfectly with RealVisXL.
Pony Realism (v2.2): While your models are standard SDXL, using realism-focused refinements like those found in the Pony ecosystem can improve specific details like hands, feet, and refined skin textures.
Detail Tweaker XL: A "utility" LoRA that allows you to increase or decrease the level of fine detail (pores, fabric weave, etc.) without changing the overall composition of your image.
Skin & Texture Specialists: Look for "Global Skin" or "Realistic Skin" LoRAs. These are designed to fix the "plastic" look AI sometimes gives and add realistic imperfections.
Recommended Settings for Maximum Realism
To get the most out of these models and LoRAs, use these technical settings:
Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras is the gold standard for Juggernaut XL to ensure sharp, clean results.
CFG Scale: Keep this between 3 and 6. Lower CFG scales (closer to 3-4) often produce more realistic, less "over-processed" results.
Steps: Aim for 30–40 steps for the best balance of detail and performance.
Resolution: For portraits, use 832 x 1216. For landscape/cinematic, use 1216 x 832.
Prompting Tip
Instead of just "photorealistic," try using "editorial film style" or "raw photo" to trigger the model's training on high-quality real-world datasets.
